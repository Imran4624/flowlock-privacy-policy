FLOW LOCK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FLOW LOCK ("the App"), an Android puzzle game published under the package name com.flowlock.flow_lock, handles information when you use the App.

By using FLOW LOCK, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue using the App.

1. Information We Collect

Guest gameplay

You can play FLOW LOCK as a guest without creating an account. Your gameplay progress, coins, settings, and scores are stored locally on your device and are not intentionally synchronized to our cloud through an account.

Account information

If you choose to create an account, we may process your email address, display name, Firebase user ID, and authentication credentials. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication rather than being stored directly by the App developer.

Cloud-synchronized gameplay data

If you sign in, your game progress, coins, unlocked levels, stars, themes, and settings may be synchronized through Firebase and Cloud Firestore. This allows you to continue playing on supported devices using your account.

Technical information

Firebase and its related infrastructure may process technical information needed for security, authentication, diagnostics, and service operation. Depending on the services and configuration used, this may include IP address, device or app identifiers, app version, and user-agent or diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used, where applicable, to:

  • Provide the game's features and functionality.
  • Save and synchronize gameplay progress when you sign in.
  • Authenticate accounts and help protect against unauthorized access or abuse.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the App.
  • Respond to support requests and account deletion requests.

3. Third-Party Services and Sharing

FLOW LOCK uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for account management and optional cloud synchronization.

Google LLC and its relevant affiliates may process information in accordance with the applicable service terms and privacy policies. You can review Google's privacy policy here: Google Privacy Policy .

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers to operate the App, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

The current release does not display advertisements or process in-app payments through the App's user interface.

4. Data Storage and Security

Guest gameplay data is stored locally on your device. When account synchronization is used, relevant account and gameplay data may be stored using Firebase services.

Data transmitted to Firebase services is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS where supported by those services. We rely on appropriate security measures to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data generally remains on your device until it is removed by you or deleted through device or App actions. Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored data, depending on your device and backup settings.

Cloud data may be retained while your account remains active or as necessary for legitimate operational or legal purposes. You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data using the contact details below.

6. Children's Privacy

FLOW LOCK is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

7. Your Choices and Account Deletion

  • You may play as a guest without creating an account.
  • You may choose whether to use account-based cloud synchronization.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated cloud data by contacting us at the email address below.

For account deletion, email us from your preferred contact address and include the email address associated with your FLOW LOCK account. We will verify the request as reasonably necessary and delete the account and associated data within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements and any necessary retention obligations.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
